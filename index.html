About

A life in service of rural India

Dr. Boraian retired as Professor of Rural Development from the Gandhigram Rural Institute (GRI), Tamil Nadu, and continues there as Visiting Emeritus Professor (Hon.).

For 40 years he has taught rural development, project management, development aid for NGOs, training of development personnel, Participatory Rural Appraisal (PRA), and project monitoring and evaluation.

Since 1985 he has offered consultancy to NGOs across South India. Since retiring he has worked on CSR assignments for public and private sector organisations in Tamil Nadu and Puducherry.
